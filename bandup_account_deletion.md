# Delete your BandUp account

You can ask us to delete your BandUp account (Android app `com.bbs.bandup`) and all data associated with it.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to [arifmoneeb02@gmail.com](mailto:arifmoneeb02@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20BandUp%20account) with the subject **"Delete my BandUp account"**.
2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to BandUp (so we can verify it is you). If you used a guest account, tell us the approximate date you last used the app and we will help you identify it.
3. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account (email and login credentials)
- All your test answers, transcripts, scores and feedback
- Your coin balance and subscription record in our system

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription – manage that in *Google Play → Payments & subscriptions*. Unused coins are forfeited on deletion.

## What is kept

Nothing personal. Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

See also the [BandUp Privacy Policy](bandup_privacy_policy.md).
