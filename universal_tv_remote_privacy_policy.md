# Privacy Policy — Universal TV Remote

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026  
**Application:** Universal TV Remote Control (`com.universalremote.app`)

Universal TV Remote ("the app") turns your phone into a remote control for your TV. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Information the app collects

The app uses **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (provided by Google) to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. They collect:

- **App activity:** which screens are opened, which features are used (for example "remote opened", "TV added", "language changed"), and whether connecting to a TV worked, along with the TV type (for example "Roku" or "Samsung").
- **App info and performance:** crash reports and error details, such as which step failed and the type of error.
- **Device information and identifiers:** phone model, Android version, app version, language, and an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase.

The app **does not** collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or audio. It does not use the advertising ID and does not show ads. Before diagnostic messages are sent, the app removes IP addresses from them.

## Information that stays on your phone

- **Your saved TVs** (names, network addresses and pairing keys) are stored only on your phone.
- **Finding TVs:** the app searches your local Wi-Fi network for compatible TVs. This traffic stays on your local network.
- **Casting:** photos, videos and music you cast are streamed directly from your phone to your TV over your local network. They are never uploaded to us.
- **Voice commands** use your phone's built-in speech recognition service. The app receives only the recognised text, uses it to send the matching command to your TV, and does not store it. The speech service is governed by its provider's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Nearby devices / local network** (Android 17 and newer): used only to find TVs on your Wi-Fi and send them commands. Android may describe this as finding and connecting to nearby devices; the app does not use it to track your location.
- **Infrared:** used to send remote-control signals from phones that have an IR blaster.
- **Internet and network state:** used to talk to your TV, cast media, and send the analytics and crash reports described above.

## Sharing

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for advertising. Data collected by Firebase is processed by Google as our service provider under the [Firebase privacy terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Security and retention

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Analytics data is kept for a limited time (2 to 14 months, as configured in Firebase) and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection. To ask about or request deletion of data linked to your app installation, contact us at the address below.
